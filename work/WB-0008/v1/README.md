# Spectrum, semimodules and projectives of the inverse semiring R_0 of Faul, Goswami, Joubert and Manuell

WB-0008, version 1. Permanent record: <https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/mathematics-workbenches/blob/main/works/WB-0008.md>.

For a nonzero commutative ring R, the semiring R_0 of Faul, Goswami, Joubert and Manuell is R with a new zero
adjoined. This work determines the prime spectrum of R_0 (Spec R with one new point below all others, where the
localization is the Boolean semifield), its congruences, its semimodules (the strong semilattices of R-modules) and
its finitely generated projective semimodules (classified by finite posets carrying a finitely generated projective
R-module at each element; their semilattices of idempotents are exactly the finite distributive lattices). For
R = Z this is Clifford's theorem with a classification of the finitely generated projective commutative inverse
monoids. R -> R_0 is fully faithful on commutative rings.

The construction began as an idea of the depositor's, in a conversation with Gemini 2.5 Pro (Gemini app); the
results were produced by Gemini 2.5 Deep Think (Gemini app, Deep Think mode) and by GPT-5-series Pro models (ChatGPT
Pro mode, at reasoning effort ultra), checked and written up by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, and checked again and
edited by Claude Fable 5.2 at effort max. The construction itself is Faul, Goswami, Joubert and Manuell's
(arXiv:2411.12318, Definition 3.6), which came first. No human has checked the proofs.
