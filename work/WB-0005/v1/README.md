# Divisibility of invariant derivatives on formal groups of every height, on a question of Katz

WB-0005, version 1, deposited on Hexagon on 23 November 2026.

In 1977 Katz asked for explicit lower bounds on the p-adic valuation of (D^n f)(0), where D is the normalized
invariant derivation of a one-parameter formal group of height h and f an integral power series. This work proves
the bound c_h n - (h-1), with c_h = 1/(p-1) - 1/(p^h-1), and the floor sum over the j not divisible by h, for every
one-dimensional formal group whose multiplication by p is divisible by p below degree p^h, over any p-torsion-free
Z_(p)-algebra and for every integral f, with the slope, the constant and the floor sum at the powers of p optimal. At
height two Katz's bound follows for every integral function except at orders divisible by p^2-1, where it can fail by
one. For the Cartier dual of the Honda formal group it constructs an integral model whose h-1 directions have the
sharp slopes p^i/(p^h-1), each with constant 1.
