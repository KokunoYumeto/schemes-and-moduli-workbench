# A very ample counterexample to Kodaira vanishing on a surface, answering a question of Raynaud

WB-0003, version 1, deposited on Hexagon on 23 November 2026.

In 1978 Raynaud gave a smooth projective surface in positive characteristic with an ample line bundle whose inverse
has nonzero first cohomology, and asked whether Kodaira vanishing can also fail for an ample bundle generated by its
sections, or even a very ample one. This work answers both questions on surfaces. On Raynaud's surface in
characteristic 2 a modified twist Q is ample and globally generated with h^1(Q^-1) = 1. Adjoining ninth roots of a
general basis of the sections of Q^9 gives, over every algebraically closed field of characteristic 2, a smooth
projective surface Y with a very ample line bundle L such that h^0(L) = 562, h^1(L^-1) = 1 and h^1(O_Y) = 565. The
passage from globally generated to very ample works in every dimension and characteristic.
